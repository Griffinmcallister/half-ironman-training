Why three bikes?

Your running base is already comparatively strong. The 90 km bike is the discipline most likely to determine whether you start the run with useful legs.

The three-bike pattern gives you:

  • one low-cost aerobic bike,
  • one quality bike,
  • one long race-development ride.

Why not three standalone runs?

You already tolerate 15–20 km long runs. More run volume would add disproportionate musculoskeletal load while competing with cycling development and lower-body strength.

Bricks gradually provide a third running exposure without requiring another weekday training slot.

Why open water by Halloween?

Early exposure is useful if it stays low stress. Your first sessions are for comfort, sighting and continuous rhythm, not fitness testing.

Pool sessions remain your best place to improve repeatable technique.

Why Friday stays empty

Saturday and Sunday contain your most important endurance work. A real recovery day protects those sessions and helps you absorb the Tuesday–Thursday cluster.
